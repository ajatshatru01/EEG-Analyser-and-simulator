# Conclusion

This project demonstrates a comprehensive approach to EEG analysis, addressing challenges related to large datasets, class imbalance, limited data availability, and manual labeling.

- Through careful data management, augmentation, advanced preprocessing, and model development, we were able to build robust classifiers capable of detecting stress, seizures, and other neural patterns.

- The lessons learned highlight the importance of synthetic data techniques, accurate labeling, and proactive data acquisition strategies in EEG research.

Looking forward, the system provides a strong foundation for real-time clinical deployment, drug effect simulation, and broader neurological studies. Overall, this work underscores the potential of EEG-based digital systems for personalized healthcare, neuroscience research, and intelligent monitoring solutions.
