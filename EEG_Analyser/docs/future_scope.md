# Future Scope

## Real-Time Clinical Deployment

Future work could focus on developing real-time EEG monitoring systems to assist clinicians in seizure and stress detection. Such systems could be integrated with hospital dashboards for continuous patient tracking and automated alerts. Additionally, exploring edge deployment on portable EEG devices could enable on-site neurofeedback and immediate interventions.

## Drug Effect Simulation

The models can be extended to simulate neural responses to pharmacological interventions, such as Carbamazepine (anti-epileptic) and Risperidone (antipsychotic). Comparing pre- and post-drug EEG bandpower changes could provide insights into drug effects, enabling potential applications in personalized medicine by predicting individual drug efficacy through EEG biomarkers.

## Research Extensions

Further research could investigate band-specific attention activations to interpret underlying physiological mechanisms. Additionally, a generalized EEG analysis framework could be developed to study emotions, cognition, and other neurological phenomena across diverse datasets and conditions.
